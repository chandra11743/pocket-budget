Privacy

Pocket Budget · updated 25 October 2026

The short version

Your expenses stay on your device and, if you choose to sign in, in your own Google Drive. There is no Pocket Budget server, no account with us, no ads and no tracking.

What is stored, and where

  • On your device: the expenses, budgets, categories and settings you enter, in the browser's storage for this site.
  • In your Google Drive (only if you sign in): one file with the same data, in a hidden app folder. It is used to back up your data and to sync your devices.

Google sign-in

Pocket Budget asks Google for one permission: drive.appdata. It lets the app create and read its own file in a hidden folder of your Drive. It does not let the app see, change or delete any of your other Drive files, your email or your contacts.

The file is private to your Google account. Nobody else can open it, including the person who made or hosts Pocket Budget. The app talks to Google directly from your device; nothing passes through another server.

The sign-in token is kept only in your browser and expires after about an hour.

Other connections

When you record money sent home in another currency, the app looks up that day's exchange rate from a public exchange-rate service (Frankfurter or ExchangeRate-API). Only the two currency codes and the date are sent.

Removing your data

  • Settings → Sync → More → Delete my data from Google Drive deletes the file from your Drive and signs you out.
  • Settings → Data → Erase … data on this device deletes it from the device.
  • You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
